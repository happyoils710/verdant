### What I’m asking

Would you serve as a **Squads multisig signer** for the Verdant treasury?

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Role | [Ops / Technical / Independent / Impact / Community / Security] |

| Networks | Practice on **Devnet**, then **Mainnet** |
| Time | Review + sign execution txs within ~72h when a passed proposal needs execution |

- A discretionary spender of the treasury (governance + policy first)

- A Solana wallet you control (hardware wallet strongly preferred on mainnet)  
- Willingness to have your **role** (and usually pubkey) listed publicly  
- Comfort rejecting spends that lack a proposal or proof plan
