# Verdant Charter

**Version:** 0.1 (Draft)  
**Status:** To be ratified on-chain as proposal **V-000**  
**Token:** Verdant (VERD)

---

## One-liner

Verdant (VERD) is a Solana governance token for a transparent community treasury that funds verifiable sustainability actions and Solana public goods with real environmental co-benefits.

---

## Purpose

Verdant exists to coordinate collective capital and decision-making so the Solana community can fund **measurable environmental outcomes** and **ecosystem health** without greenwashing.

The treasury’s job is to spend — carefully, publicly, and with proof — on:

1. Verifiable sustainability actions  
2. Solana ecosystem public goods that have sustainability co-benefits  
3. Integrity infrastructure (measurement, reporting, verification, transparency tooling)

---

## What VERD is

- A **governance token** used to propose and vote on treasury policy and spends  
- A coordination mechanism for a **transparent, multi-signature-controlled treasury**  
- Fixed supply; impact is funded from treasury assets, **not** by printing tokens  

## What VERD is not

- Not a representation that holding VERD makes a wallet, user, or the Solana network “carbon neutral” or “climate positive”  
- Not an automatic offset of blockchain activity  
- Not a promise of profit, dividends, or investment return  
- Not a substitute for legal, tax, or regulated environmental compliance  

---

## Treasury mandate

### In scope

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Verifiable sustainability** | High-integrity carbon or biodiversity credits with public retirement; RECs; support for on-chain climate protocols with clear deliverables |
| **Ecosystem health + co-benefits** | Open-source tools, climate-related infrastructure, relevant DePIN pilots, education, public goods that strengthen Solana and environmental capacity |
| **Integrity infrastructure** | MRV tooling, open dashboards, reporting systems, audits of process (not hype reports) |
| **Necessary ops** | Legal, security, basic infrastructure — capped and disclosed |

### Out of scope (v0.1)

- Speculative trading programs as “impact”  
- Marketing spends framed as climate action  
- Unverifiable tree-planting or donation screenshots as sole evidence  
- Any claim that token price equals environmental progress  

---

## Principles

1. **Proof over claims** — No impact narrative without public evidence.  
2. **On-chain accountability** — Material spends via multisig/DAO with memos, linked reports, and periodic accounting.  
3. **No greenwash** — Official channels do not market “holding VERD = climate positive.”  
4. **Fixed supply** — No inflationary mint “for impact.”  
5. **Community control** — Material treasury actions require governance, except narrowly defined emergency policy under multisig limits.  
6. **Honesty about uncertainty** — Prefer smaller verified actions over large vague promises.  

---

## Governance relationship

- **VERD holders** vote on proposals (parameters per `docs/governance-setup.md`).  
- **Squads multisig** holds and executes treasury assets according to passed proposals and published policy.  
- **Documents in this repo** are the off-chain constitution until amended by governance.  

---

## Transparency obligations

| Cadence | Obligation |
|---------|------------|
| Every material spend | Proposal + execution tx(s) + proof package in `proofs/` |
| Monthly | Treasury balances snapshot |
| Quarterly | Impact & spend report (even if spend is zero) |
| Annually | Policy review (eligible assets, quorum, signer set) |
