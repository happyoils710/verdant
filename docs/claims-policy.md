# Verdant Claims Policy

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Binding for official Verdant channels (site, GitHub, Realms, socials operated by the project)

This policy protects long-term legitimacy. Greenwashing accusations destroy governance tokens faster than low liquidity.

---

## What VERD is (approved language)

You **may** say:

- VERD is a **Solana governance token**.  
- VERD holders govern a **transparent community treasury**.  
- The treasury **aims to fund** verifiable sustainability actions and Solana public goods with environmental co-benefits.  
- Impact occurs **only when** the treasury executes approved spends and publishes proof.  
- Verdant prioritizes **proof over claims** and **on-chain accountability**.  

### Approved one-liner

> Verdant (VERD): Solana governance for a transparent treasury funding verifiable sustainability actions and Solana public goods with environmental co-benefits. Proof required. No greenwash.

---

## Forbidden claims (do not use)

Official channels **must not** claim or imply:

| Forbidden | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| “Carbon-negative token” / “climate-positive token” | Holding a token is not an environmental outcome |
| “Holding VERD offsets your footprint” | False mechanism |
| “We offset Solana” / “We green the blockchain” | Overclaim; Solana efficiency ≠ Verdant retirement |
| “Guaranteed APY” / “risk-free green yield” | Misleading financial + impact framing |
| “Every trade plants a tree” / unaudited auto-impact | Unverifiable by default |
| “SEC-approved” / “compliance guaranteed” | Unless literally true with counsel sign-off |
| “Backed by real carbon” as if redeemable 1:1 | Treasury may *hold or retire* assets; VERD is not a warehouse receipt unless a future structure explicitly is |

---

## Conditional claims (only with proof)

You **may** say these **only after** the referenced action is done and linked:

| Claim shape | Requires |
|-------------|----------|
| “Verdant Treasury retired X tonnes under registry Y” | Serials + retirement proof + payment tx + `proofs/` link |
| “Verdant funded grant Z; milestone 1 delivered” | Proposal + tx + public deliverable |
| “In Qn we spent $A on category B” | Quarterly report |

**Rule:** Past tense + link to proof. No forward-looking “we will save the planet.”

---

## What VERD is not (approved clarifying language)

Use freely:

- VERD is **not** financial advice or an investment solicitation.  
- VERD is **not** a claim on pro-rata treasury redemption in v0.1.  
- Environmental outcomes are **not guaranteed**.  
- Unofficial community statements are **not** Verdant policy.

## Channel rules

| Channel | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Token metadata description | Use approved one-liner only |
| Website hero | Governance + treasury + proof — not price |
| Twitter/X / Discord | Moderators correct greenwash memes from “official” tone |
| Partner quotes | Must not put forbidden claims in Verdant’s mouth |
| Influencers | No paid push that uses forbidden claims; prefer no paid hype at all in v0.1 |

---

## Correction process

If an official post overclaims:

1. Delete or correct within **24 hours** if possible  
2. Post a clear correction with accurate language  
3. Note in monthly transparency log if material  

---

## Team / partner personal accounts

Contributors should avoid implying personal holdings equal personal carbon neutrality “via Verdant.” Prefer: “I participate in Verdant governance; impact is from treasury actions [link].”

---

## Amendments

Update via Policy proposal. When in doubt, **under-claim and over-document**.
