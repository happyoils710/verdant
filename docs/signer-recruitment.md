## Role targets

| Seat | Role | Who to ask |
|------|------|------------|
| 1 | Ops / coordinator | You (core) |
| 2 | Technical | Solana builder / engineer friend |
| 3 | Technical backup | Second engineer or infra person |
| 4 | Independent | Someone with **no** token allocation conflict if possible |
| 5 | Impact / domain | Climate, MRV, energy, or registry-adjacent person |
| 6 | Community / public goods | OSS / DAO / grants experienced person |

Minimum viable for **practice** multisig: seats 1–3.  
Minimum for **mainnet legitimacy:** at least **5** confirmed, including **one independent** and **one impact/domain**.

## Compensation policy (v0.1 recommendation)

- **No cash** for signing  
- Optional: small VERD from **team/partners** vest only if disclosed and not from Community Treasury  
- Never pay signers from Impact vault  

---

## Red flags (do not appoint)

- Will not use their own wallet / wants shared seed  
- Demands large unlocked VERD to join  
- Wants discretionary spend without votes  
- Unreachable for &gt;1 week regularly without backup plan
