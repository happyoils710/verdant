# Stuck recruiting signers? Read this.

Cold “will you be on our multisig?” has a **low hit rate**. That does not mean Verdant is dead. It means the ask is high-trust and you are early.

---

## Why people ignore or decline

| Reason | What they think |
|--------|------------------|
| Liability anxiety | “If something goes wrong, am I on the hook?” |
| Time / notification fatigue | “Another wallet to babysit” |
| Unknown project | “Who are you and will this be a scam brand?” |
| Token association | “I don’t want my name next to a token” |
| No relationship | Cold DM = default no |
| Ambiguous upside | Volunteer + no story yet |

You’re not failing the product. You’re hitting a **cold-start trust** problem.

---

## What actually works (ranked)

1. **Warm intros only** — people who already trust you  
2. **In-person / voice** — 10-minute call beats a perfect GitHub page  
3. **Smaller ask first** — “review our spend policy?” then later “co-sign?”  
4. **Shared mission** — climate/OSS people who care about *process*, not tokens  
5. **Public post** — lowest conversion; keep it, don’t rely on it  

If public posts got zero DMs, **stop optimizing the post**. Optimize **who you already know**.

---

## Fallback path (recommended if stuck)

Do **not** wait forever for 7 famous signers.

### Path A — Honest interim multisig (best)

| Stage | Structure | Public message |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| Now | **2-of-3** or **3-of-5** with people who actually said yes | “Interim treasury multisig; expanding to 4-of-7” |
| Later | Expand seats as people join | Publish roster updates |

**Rules for interim:**

- At least **one key is not yours** (if only you: do not call it a DAO multisig)  
- Publish **roles + pubkeys**  
- Write in README: *interim · will expand*  
- No large Impact spends until ≥3 independent-ish humans  

### Path B — You + hardware + delayed expansion

- Mainnet mint with long vests, small personal runway  
- Impact vault **empty** until multisig is multi-person  
- Public commitment: “No V-001 until N external signers”  

Still legitimate if you **don’t overclaim** decentralization.

### Path C — Partner entity as co-signer

- Small OSS project, local climate nonprofit lead, or co-founder friend  
- One org-controlled wallet (still one human in practice — disclose that)

## Rewrite the ask (higher yes rate)

### Too heavy (low yes)

> Join our 4-of-7 DAO multisig for a climate governance token.

### Lighter (higher yes)

> I need a second/third set of eyes on a Solana treasury wallet.  
> Low volume, public process, you can refuse any tx.  
> Not asking you to promote anything. 15 min to set up.

### Even lighter (foot in the door)

> Would you skim our spend/claims policy and tell me if anything looks greenwashy?  
> (No wallet required yet.)

Convert skimmers → signers later.

## What not to do when stuck

- Bribe with large unlocked VERD  
- Fill seats with your own burner wallets  
- Claim “community multisig” with 1-of-1  
- Spam 50 Discords harder  
- Delay the whole project forever waiting for a big name

## Minimum viable trust for mainnet

| Level | Multisig | What you may do |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| Weak | 1-of-1 you | Build docs, Devnet only, or mainnet **without** calling it decentralized treasury |
| OK interim | 2-of-3 (you + 2 real people) | Mainnet mint, small ops; delay V-001 if you want |
| Solid | 3-of-5 | V-000, careful V-001 |
| Target | 4-of-7 | Steady state |
