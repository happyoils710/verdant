# Initial Treasury Cash Plan (USDC / SOL)

**Version:** 0.1  
**Purpose:** V-001 and ops need real cash, not only VERD. This document plans a **small, disclosed seed** without greenwash or investment promises.

---

## Principle

- VERD governs; **USDC/SOL executes impact**.  
- Seed funds are **donations/seeds to the treasury**, not purchases of impact for holders.  
- Publish every seed transfer on-chain + in the monthly snapshot.

---

## Recommended minimum seed (pre–V-001)

| Bucket | Amount (USDC equiv.) | Use |
|--------|----------------------|-----|
| **Impact** | **$500–1,000** | V-001 retirement + small buffer |
| **Ops** | **$200–500** | Legal docs, domain, security tools, fees |
| **Public Goods** | $0 initially | Fund after V-001 via later seeds/grants |
| **Total** | **~$700–1,500** | Enough for legitimacy without overclaiming |

Scale up only after proof packs exist.

---

## Source options (pick one and disclose)

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **A. Founder personal seed** | Fast, clean story | Personal cost |
| **B. Multiple small community seeds** | Shared ownership | Coordination |
| **C. External grant to treasury** | Non-dilutive | Slower |

**v0.1 recommendation:** **Option A** for V-001 speed, labeled clearly as treasury seed (not investment).

---

## Disclosure template (for seed tx memo / snapshot)

```text
Verdant Treasury seed — Impact vault — for V-001 execution capacity.
Not a sale of tokens. Not a claim of environmental outcome.
```

---

## What we will not do

- Sell VERD as “carbon credits”  
- Promise seeders any offset attributed to their wallet  
- Comingle personal trading funds with treasury without labeling  

---
