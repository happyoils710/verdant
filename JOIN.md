# Join Verdant

We’re recruiting **independent multisig co-signers** for the Verdant treasury (Solana / Squads).

## What Verdant is

A **governance token** for a transparent community treasury that funds:

1. **Verifiable sustainability actions** (public proof required — e.g. retirement serials)
2. **Solana public goods** with real environmental co-benefits

Impact comes only from executed treasury spends. **Holding VERD is not a carbon offset.**

- [Claims policy](./docs/claims-policy.md) (what we never claim)
- [Spend policy](./docs/spend-policy.md)

## What we’re looking for

| Seat | Profile |
|------|---------|
| Technical | Solana / systems engineer |
| Independent | Not a founder; willing to refuse bad spends |
| Impact / domain | Climate, energy, MRV, or related |
| Community / public goods | OSS, grants, ecosystem experience |
| Security-minded | Key hygiene, process discipline |

**Structure:** target **4-of-7** Squads multisig  
**Time:** low — roughly respond within ~72h when a signature is needed  
**Pay:** none by default (no unlocked tokens for joining)  
**Identity:** stable public handle + wallet you alone control; legal name optional  
**Not wanted:** shills, shared keys, or “hire my wallet” services  

## How to apply

Open a **GitHub Issue** with title:

```text
Signer interest: [tech | independent | impact | community | security]
```

Include:

1. Seat of interest  
2. Short background or links (GitHub, X, site)  
3. Mainnet Solana pubkey (or “will send after chat”)  
4. Confirmation you read the claims policy  

Or contact the maintainer via the channels listed on their GitHub profile.
